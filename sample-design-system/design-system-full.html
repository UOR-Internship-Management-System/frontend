CV Management & Internship System

A Material Design 3 Expressive system built for a role-aware academic platform — bolder shape and type for the parts students touch daily, calmer density for the tables and forms administrators live in.

Color

Three tonal families: Scholar Violet (primary actions), Ledger Amber (achievement/CV content), Verified Teal (confirmation states) — plus a neutral surface family with five container steps for layering.

Typography
